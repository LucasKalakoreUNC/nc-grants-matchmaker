Two Honest Failures & The Engineering Fixes (§6)

Judges Scoring Requirement

Judges reward honest evidence over false perfection. Here are real boundary failures encountered during testing and the algorithms implemented to resolve them:

Failure 1: False Positive Fixed

Superfund Hazardous Substance Research (NIH Clinical Trial)

Root Cause: High keyword match on "Superfund" and "Hazardous Substances" matched NC DEQ Goal 3, but the grant was strictly intended for university medical schools with clinical patient trials.

Algorithm Fix: Implemented applicant type validation and negative scoring penalty for "Clinical Trial" to prevent misdirecting state remediation staff.

Failure 2: False Negative Fixed

Aquifer Resilience and Emerging Solute Transport Modeling

Root Cause: Opportunity omitted standard buzzwords ("drinking water", "PFAS") and used specialized hydrogeology phrasing ("solute transport"), causing initial keyword score to fall below threshold.

Algorithm Fix: Augmented profile goals with semantic domain thesaurus linking hydrogeological terms directly into NC DEQ Goal 1 (Water Quality).

GenAI Field Notes: Prompting & Verification (§7.3)

Documenting AI hallucination safeguards as required for final competition deliverables:

Where AI Was Wrong: During initial summary testing, the LLM inferred that North Carolina state agencies had an automatic 50% non-federal cash match requirement on all federal awards.

How We Caught It: Human-in-the-loop spot checks against actual Grants.gov synopsis fields revealed cost-sharing varies by specific federal statute (frequently 0% or 20%).

Prompt Constraint Implemented: Constrained summarizer prompts to strictly quote the official costSharing: true/false field from Grants.gov rather than conjecturing matching percentages.
